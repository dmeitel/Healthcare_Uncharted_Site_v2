With Linked on, either frame pulls the other to the same page and the same place on it. ← → step through pages, r reloads, t toggles theme, m (or Ctrl+Enter in the notes) saves the notes and the mark for the theme on screen: at night it then shows day, in day the next page. Live reloads both frames by itself when the page is rebuilt. The phone frame is a real viewport at that width, not a scaled screenshot. Each page takes two marks, Night and Day, each with its notes from the bar under the header; a mark turns amber when the page changed after it.
