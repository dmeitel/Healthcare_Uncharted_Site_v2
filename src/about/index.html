Who Built the Map, and Why

Who built this map

The clinical, financial, operational, and political layers of healthcare get mapped separately, when they get mapped at all. Healthcare Uncharted draws them on one chart. This page is the who and the why.

The site

About Healthcare Uncharted

Mission
"Healthcare runs on clinical, financial, operational, and policy layers that rarely share a page. Healthcare Uncharted puts them on one chart and draws the lines between them, for anyone whose job depends on seeing the whole thing."

Healthcare technology is full of territory that nobody maps end-to-end. A clinician charts in an enterprise EHR every shift and is never told why the data is shaped the way it is. The analyst downstream writes SQL against those same tables without the clinical meaning. And the leadership team? They are signing AI contracts with no reliable way to tell what works from what is still a demo.

Healthcare Uncharted is the system map I wish had existed when I started in healthcare informatics. It is built from real clinical bedside experience, real health administration, and real work with EHR data systems. Not vendor training materials or theoretical frameworks.

The goal is simple: chart how the pieces connect, and write it so any seat in the hospital can use it.
