The mission

Map the connections, not just the components.

Healthcare technology is full of territory nobody maps in plain language. Clinicians use systems nobody explains to them. Analysts query the data without its clinical meaning. Leaders sign AI contracts on the vendor's word. Healthcare Uncharted exists to close that gap, for every role in the building, clinical and business alike. Built from real bedside and informatics experience.
