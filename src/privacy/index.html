Privacy

No accounts, no ads and no cookies. Nothing you type into a tool leaves your browser. The rest is below, one service at a time.

Counting visits

The site counts visits with GoatCounter. It records which page was opened, the browser and system, a country, the language, the screen width and the page you came from, and keeps them as daily totals. It sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses. So that one person is not counted twice, it matches repeat visits in memory for up to eight hours, then forgets them. GoatCounter's privacy policy has the rest.

The email list

There is no email signup on the site right now; the Field Notes form came down on October 11, 2026. Addresses left before then are kept by Netlify, the site's host, and are used for one thing only: a note when there is a new tool or new data. To have yours removed, ask through the About page.

What stays in your browser

Your light or dark choice, your place in a module, your game progress and the choices you make in a tool are saved in your browser's own storage, on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Playing with other people

When you open or join a game table, the name you type and your moves are relayed to the other players through Supabase, a realtime service. The table is not saved; when everyone leaves, it is gone.

Your location and your files

The two U.S. maps have a locate button. Your browser asks your permission first, and the position is used on your screen to move the map. It is not sent to this site or to anyone else.

The Career Tree can read a LinkedIn profile saved as a PDF to fill in your path. The file is read inside your browser and is never uploaded.

The Cost of Living Comparison looks up federal per diem rates. It sends only a state and a year, through the site's own relay, to the GSA's per diem service.

Other servers your browser talks to

Like any web page, these pages load files from other servers, and each request shows that server your IP address. The servers are Netlify, the host, which keeps ordinary request logs; Google Fonts, for the typefaces; jsDelivr and cdnjs, for code libraries; OpenFreeMap, for the map tiles; and Google Maps, on the one page that embeds a map.

Questions

Ask through the About page. The date at the top of this page is the last time it was checked against the code.
