The Secret Menu

You found it. Eight taps on the compass, or you just knew the address. This is the part of the site that isn't on the site: no nav link points here, the sitemap doesn't list it, search engines are told to look away.

What this is
A back room. Half-finished ideas, experiments that aren't ready for the front page, and the occasional thing that's just for people paying attention. It works like Learn. It's just quieter. Check back, the menu changes.
─ On the Menulive right now
Dev BlogBlog
Notes from building this site, every couple of updates: what we figured out, what broke, ideas for what comes next, and the feedback we heard, with a record of what came of it. Post 1 is the 1.0 release.
Uncharted GeneralGame
A healthcare systems roguelite. Run a hospital network quarter by quarter. Set pay, move staff, open units, then run the quarter and see if you hit the goal. Clear it, pick an upgrade. Miss it, the run's over. It gets meaner every level.
ER Charge NurseGame
Twelve hours in twelve minutes. You run the board, not the bedside: place patients, watch the queues, spend six charge cards on the right crises. Overcooked energy, emergency-department physics. The smallest lens in the game ladder.
Uncharted RegionalGame
The sequel lens to Uncharted General: the hospital becomes a card. Run three facilities in one region, one shared workforce pool, one payer table. Centralize a service line and the margin thanks you before the community does.
Camp NauvooVision
A long-term plan to buy an 86-acre camp in the Sierra foothills and run it as a nonprofit: respite for burned-out clinicians, last good days outdoors for people near the end of life, and public camping that keeps it all running.
Data ObservatoryInstrument
The site's plumbing, drawn. Every external source, every pipeline script, every dataset and the tools that read them, laid out as one flow map. Hover a node and its whole chain lights up. The second tab is the traffic, from the Goat Tracker.
Utah Hospital Price FinderInstrument
Every hospital has to publish its full price list. We pulled Utah's, all 8,500 billable codes across 43 hospitals, including the 43-gigabyte University of Utah file. Search any service or code and see the cash price at every hospital that posts one, cheapest first.

Patient PopulationsReading
The twenty states a person can be in when healthcare meets them, across three journeys that run alongside each other: acuity, maternal, and newborn. Sorted by what the system has to do next rather than by diagnosis or department.
