How AI is used here

A site about AI in healthcare should say how it uses AI. So here it is.

What it does

Most of the code on this site is written with Claude, an AI model, working as a coding assistant. It also drafts research, runs the checks and builds pages to a written standard. Copy often starts as an AI draft written to my voice notes, and I edit it until it says what I mean.

What it never touches

No patient information. Every page is scanned for anything that could identify a patient, and the build fails if it finds one.

No unchecked numbers. Every outside figure is checked against its source before it ships, and data on the site carries the date it was checked.

Nothing ships without me. I read the work, I commit it and I push it to the live site myself.

Why say so

Informaticists ask how a tool was built before they trust it, and they should. If you want to know how something specific was made, ask through the About page.
