Disclaimer

What this site is for, whose views these are, and what the games leave out.

Not medical advice

Everything here is for learning. None of it is medical advice, and none of it is a protocol for patient care. If you have a question about your own health or a patient's, ask the clinician responsible.

The author's own views

David Eitel works in clinical informatics at a health system. What he writes here is his own, and it does not represent his employer or any organization he works with. No employer's internal material, screenshots or data appear on this site.

Vendor and product names

Names of vendors, systems and products come from their public documentation. The site does not reproduce proprietary materials, internal training content or licensed documentation, and naming a product is not an endorsement.

The games

The games are simplified models built to teach how care runs. Their equipment setups, alarms, prices and numbers are simplified, and they are not instructions for setting up equipment on a real patient.
