Terms of use

Free to use, free to share, built for learning. A few plain terms come with that.

What this site is

Free tools, games and writing about how healthcare runs, for education. It is not clinical, legal or financial advice, and nothing here replaces a clinician, your employer's policy or a professional who knows your situation. The disclaimer says more.

The data

The tools are built on public sources, named on each page with the date they were checked. Sources change and mistakes happen, so check a number against its source before you lean on it. If you find an error, say so through the About page and it gets fixed.

Using the work

Link to anything and share it freely. Quote it with credit and a link back. The writing, tools and art belong to the author, so please do not republish whole pages as your own.

As it is

Everything here is offered as it is, without a warranty of any kind, and the author is not liable for decisions made from it. Links to other sites are there for reference and are not an endorsement.

Changes

These terms can change. The date at the top says when they last did.
