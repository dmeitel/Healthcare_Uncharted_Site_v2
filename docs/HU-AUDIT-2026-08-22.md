## 2 · A METHOD NOTE THAT COST ACCURACY

The impeccable detector, given `src/tools/` as a DIRECTORY, scans only `.html`. Five
of the eight tools are `.njk`, so the first pass silently skipped them and reported
178 findings. Re-run with explicit file paths: **572**.

Always pass tool files explicitly:

```
node .claude/skills/impeccable/scripts/detect.mjs \
  src/atlas/index.njk src/tools/iceberg-map/index.njk src/tools/multi-lens-map/index.njk \
  src/tools/career-tree/index.njk src/tools/assignment-compass/index.njk \
  src/tools/operators-map/index.html src/tools/vendor-directory/index.html \
  src/tools/hospital-map/index.html src/assets/css/hu-global.css
```

URL scanning needs puppeteer, which is NOT installed and was deliberately not added.
Runtime numbers below were measured directly in the browser instead.

## 3 · FIXED

**Iceberg ran two phone lines.** 699px at lines 42 and 114, 768px at 255 and 442, both
768 blocks explicitly phone-intent. Between 700 and 768px wide it applied half its
phone layout and not the other half. Now 699 throughout. Verified at 720px (desktop
branch: `#rp` relative, `#as` absolute, padding 14px) and 390px (phone branch: `#rp`
fixed, `#as` static, padding 0).

**A hand-rolled twin.** `.lv-sw` and `.gv-sw` were byte-identical apart from the
prefix, confirmed by diff. Both animated the knob with `left`. Now one `.hu-sw` in
hu-global.css riding `transform: translateX(15px)`, same 15px travel, plus the
`:focus-visible` ring and reduced-motion guard neither copy had. Both are
`role="switch"` spans with `tabindex="0"`, so they were keyboard-reachable with NO
visible focus indicator.

**Iceberg's bottom sheet clears the home indicator.** `#rp.rp-open` gained
`padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and `55vh` gained a `55dvh` sibling.

**Three contrast failures were token drift, not design.** `--t3` (#7E9CBF), documented
in the CSS as "muted, meta only", measures **6.28:1** on the card ground. It passes
comfortably. Three selectors had escaped it:

| | Hand-rolled | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.bp-dash-lbl` | `rgba(160,195,210,0.5)` | 3.32 | **6.75** |
| `.bp-hint` | `rgba(180,205,215,0.5)` | 3.60 | **6.28** |
| `.bp-next-empty` | `rgba(180,205,215,0.55)` | 4.09 | **6.28** |

Light theme already routed all three through `--t2` and was untouched.

**A destructive control at 14x15.** `.bp-card-x` ("Remove section") drew three times
under the touch floor. The glyph is unchanged; a centred 44x44 invisible pad now
carries the target. Verified: 54 of 81 sampled points resolve to the button, the 9
that do not land on a container rather than another control, no neighbour stolen.

> **Note for future audits:** this will STILL report as 14x15.
> `getBoundingClientRect()` does not count an absolutely-positioned pseudo-element.
> The tappable area is genuinely 44x44; a naive measurement cannot see it.

**Hospital map's accent bar.** Drawn at 5px and scaled to `.6` at rest, so it grows on
`transform` instead of `height`. Measured 3px at rest and 5px on hover: pixel-identical
to before.

**A dead transition.** `.bp-node` animated `margin-top`, which nothing in any state and
no JS ever sets. Removed.

Detector warnings across the eight tools: **6 to 4.**

## 5 · OPEN · these are STANDARDS QUESTIONS, not leftover defects

Each one is a place where a written rule and the shipped design disagree. The question
is always the same: does the design move, or does the rule? There is precedent for
both. The vendor-directory `width` transition was ledgered with a written engineering
reason, so the rule bent. The teal-ink migration moved roughly 75 sites, so the design
bent. Neither is the default.

### A · How is the 44px floor met?

Eight components remain, all short in HEIGHT, not width. Eighteen elements, but only
nine distinct components, so nine fixes rather than eighteen.

| Component | Size | Short by | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bp-card-x` | 14x15 | 29 | FIXED |
| `bp-h-link` | 99x18 | 26 | 3 |
| `bp-addbtn` | 100x25 | 19 | 1 |
| `bp-guide` | 256x26 | 18 | 4 |
| `bp-id-name` | 210x28 | 16 | 1 |
| `bp-id-goal-in` | 250x28 | 16 | 1 |
| `bp-pick-in` | 342x29 | 15 | 4 |
| `ta-toggle` | 38x38 | 6 | site chrome |
| `nav-brand` | 192x40 | 4 | site chrome |

Grow the visuals (honest, makes a deliberately dense planning panel taller on phones),
grow only the hit area (costs nothing visually, the `bp-card-x` fix is a working
example), or split it by control type.

### B · Are quiet accent labels allowed to be quiet?

Iceberg's `.rh` and `.ts` are uppercase micro-labels in teal at 60% alpha, scoring
**4.20** and **4.22** against a 4.5 requirement. They miss by hundredths.

Full-strength `--teal` scores **9.22**, so the fix is trivial and makes those labels
considerably LOUDER than drawn. Alternatives: a documented "quiet teal" token, which
means adding a colour and is David's call by rule, or `--t3`, which passes but drops
the teal accent entirely.

### C · Is 11px a floor or a guideline?

Five elements sit under it on iceberg. `.sm-tag` ("MAP") is **7.5px**. `.ml` at 9.5px
uses `--t2` correctly, so it is purely a size question and not drift. Iceberg is a
dense eight-layer diagram; raising everything to 11px reflows it. Either the rule wins
and the diagram gets rebalanced, or the rule gains a documented exemption for
badge-tier micro-type, the way counter-scaled SVG canvas text already has one.
