## Body

I'm a respiratory therapist turned clinical informatics manager, and I built a clicker about the thing nobody puts on the dashboard: alarm fatigue.

You start with one patient and one monitor. You buy more responsibility: more beds, telemetry, pulse ox, a central monitoring station, an in basket, secure chat, techs who grab tasks for you. Every upgrade makes the number go up and adds another sound you have to tell apart from the others. The one that matters arrives in the same voice as the ones that did not. By the afternoon your ears have made a decision for you.

What's in it: a 12-hour shift with a clock, a code you have to run, an EHR you can reskin (three templates and nine palettes, some of them unlocked by achievements), 47 achievements, an endless mode after lunch if you want to keep grinding, and experience that banks between shifts. Techs idle if you let them and it costs you. The audio wears down the more you silence it, which is the point.

It's free, it runs in the browser, no login, no ads, no app. I built it myself and there's no money in it. It is best on a laptop; it plays on a phone but the floor is tight there.
