## Body

I'm an RT (RRT, MHA, MSRT), I run a clinical informatics team now, and I still remember the setup mistakes from my first clinicals better than anything from the book. The flowmeter nobody turned back on after the neb. The humidifier that never got plugged in. The tubing on the air outlet.

So I built a game about the wall behind the bed. Real equipment, real connector standards (22 mm, 15 mm, the barb, the DISS fittings), the outlets on the rail, the patient at the bottom, one order clipped beside them. You drag the parts out of the cart, run the tubing, and submit. It tells you what happened in a therapist's words, not the exam's: no flow, no humidity, no seal, and why.

There are five tutorials, six levels (humidified oxygen, 2 L cannula or 15 L non-rebreather, high flow, trach collar, bubble CPAP, capnography), a timed one, and three "find the fault" walls where the setup arrives built and broken and you fix it with what's on the wall. The picture is one of those. Nurses and EMS, the 2 L cannula and the 15 L NRB are in there too.

It's free, it runs in the browser, no login, no app. Built it myself, no money involved, and I'll answer every comment here. It plays best on a laptop; the phone version is coming.
