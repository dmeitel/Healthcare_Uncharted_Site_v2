# Device Assembly · a year of daily walls (brainstorm)

2026-10-08. A brainstorm, nothing built. David, after trying the race and the daily: "start considering long term
how we can come up with maybe 300 unique puzzles ... changes the orientation of the wall maybe putting the plugs at
different ends or putting the patient in the center maybe flipping things upside down putting more barriers or only
specific pathways ... sometimes they need to deconstruct some things or change the order around ... consider
literally every fun possibility of puzzle management". He picks from this after the ventilator QA.

## What the daily varies today

The dealer has nine hospital orders (cannula, humidified cannula, simple mask, non-rebreather, Venturi, trach collar,
aerosol mask, heated high flow, capnography) and nine twists (plain, crowded, the patient slid down, the Chemetron
wing, no air, no oxygen, only air, only suction, bring the pump). Then rehab, home and the ICU, seven vent orders, the
shift's complications and the fault walls. Every wall is already unique by its code.

The problem David is pointing at is different. Most days the shape of the job is the same: outlets along the top,
the patient lower left, grab four or five parts and join them. The twists mostly change where the oxygen comes
from. They rarely change what the puzzle asks you to DO.

## How a year comes out of it

A new rule every day means a new lesson every day, and nobody learns a game that way. Wordle works because the
rule holds still and the puzzle changes. So a day here would be three things put together:

- a MODE, from the catalog below: what kind of problem it is;
- an ORDER: what the patient needs (the nine hospital orders, the vent orders, the home and rehab ones, and any new
  ones, like suction or transport);
- a ROOM: where it happens (the layouts in family 1).

With 150 modes, 20 orders and 20 rooms, no two days of the year need to match, and no mode has to come back for
weeks. A rough weekly rhythm, easy early in the week and hard on Saturday, the way a newspaper crossword does it:

| Day | What it is | Families it draws from |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | A plain build in a new room | 1 (the wall moves) |
| Tuesday | The cart is the problem | 3 (supply) |
| Wednesday | The order is a riddle | 4 (clinical reasoning) |
| Thursday | Paths and barriers | 2 (obstacles), 6 (board rules) |
| Friday | Take it apart | 5 (deconstruct and rebuild) |
| Saturday | Insane: two twists stacked, the joke days | anything marked Joke, or two modes at once |
| Sunday | A story or a sequence | 8 (several patients), 9 (order of steps), 11 (story formats) |

New mechanics would arrive one or two a month, with a short teaching wall the first time they show up, so the
year gets harder as the players do.

## The catalog

Kind: REAL is something that happens on a floor, STRETCH could happen but is pushed, JOKE is the "completely
insane" end David asked for in the expansion plan. Size: SMALL is a new room or twist the dealer can already use,
MEDIUM is a new check in the test or a few new parts, BIG is a new way to play. Ideas marked HAVE already exist in some form.

## Where I'd start

If the year gets built, these first ten give the most new kinds of day for the least new machinery, and they cover
every word in David's ask:

1. Patient in the middle (1), the side wall (4), the corner room (6), prone (16). The plugs at different ends,
   the patient in the center, and the room turned around.
2. Upside down (3). He asked for it by name, and it's the Saturday joke the rest of the week sets up.
3. Tubing clips (32) and the curtain (25). Only specific pathways.
4. Lean it (92), room turnover (95), wrong order in line (94). Taking things apart and changing the order around.
5. The goal, not the device (65). The best teaching day in the list. It's what an RT actually does with an order.
6. Hot swap (100). The biggest build here, and one I haven't seen another puzzle game do.

Under the whole thing, a test that holds every day of the year to being solvable, different from every other day,
and in its difficulty band, and a year sheet on the hub's Puzzles screen with every day pictured. The same machinery
the puzzle sheet uses now.

## Questions for after the ventilator QA

1. How hard should the daily be? I recommend a weekly climb, easy Monday to hard Saturday, with Sunday as the
   story day, like a newspaper crossword.
2. How many joke days? I recommend Saturdays only, so the joke lands and the other six days teach real setups.
3. Do new rules show up cold? I recommend no. A new mode gets a one-wall teach the first time, and the year
   brings in one or two a month.
